Mission

The bridge between children's choirs and adult ensembles

Founded in 1982, the Ottawa Regional Youth Choir (ORYC) is one of Canada's leading youth choirs. About 50 singers aged 15 to 25 come together from across the National Capital Region in pursuit of artistic excellence, rehearsing on Tuesday evenings at St. Joseph's Parish in Sandy Hill and performing across Ottawa.

The ORYC performs a broad range of choral repertoire spanning many centuries, languages, cultures, and musical traditions, from Messiah and the Mozart and Brahms Requiems to Monteverdi's Vespers and Palmeri's MisaTango. Alongside these masterworks, the ORYC is committed to performing works by Canadian composers, presenting premieres, and commissioning new music, including works by our own choristers. The choir has performed with the National Arts Centre Orchestra, the Ottawa Symphony Orchestra, the Ottawa Choral Society, Music and Beyond, Capital Chamber Choir, and Cantata Singers of Ottawa.

Vision

To become the most recognized, respected, inclusive and well-funded youth choir in Canada.

In memoriam

Barbara Clark, C.M.

Died August 5, 2025

Barbara Clark founded the Ottawa Regional Youth Choir in 1982 and led it until 2007. Her vision was a choir where young singers could keep growing between children's choirs and adult ensembles. She was appointed a Member of the Order of Canada in 2001.
