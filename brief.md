# Project: Ottawa Regional Youth Choir (ORYC) — website redesign (oryc.ca)

Design a complete, multi-page marketing website for the Ottawa Regional Youth Choir. This replaces the current WordPress/Elementor site at oryc.ca. Deliver high-fidelity desktop and mobile designs for every page listed below. Use the attached brand assets and follow the brand guide exactly. All content below is real; use it verbatim where given and do not invent facts, people, dates or prices.

## 1. Who ORYC is

- Ottawa Regional Youth Choir Society (ORYC). Registered charity #11907 1827 RR0001.
- Founded 1982 by Barbara Clark, C.M.; incorporated 1983. Celebrating more than 40 years.
- SATB choir of about 50 singers aged 14–25 [CONFIRM: some pages say 15–25]. It is the bridge between children's choirs and adult ensembles.
- **Non-auditioned.** New singers book a short, no-preparation vocal check and feedback session instead of an audition. Never use the word "audition" anywhere on the site.
- Rehearses Tuesdays 7–9 pm at St. Joseph's Parish, 174 Wilbrod St., Ottawa (Sandy Hill). Performs there and at other Ottawa venues.
- Repertoire: the choral-symphonic canon (Messiah, Mozart Requiem, Brahms Requiem, Monteverdi Vespers, Palmeri MisaTango) balanced with contemporary Canadian works, including premieres by its own choristers.
- Collaborators: National Arts Centre Orchestra, Ottawa Symphony Orchestra, Ottawa Choral Society, Music and Beyond, Capital Chamber Choir, Cantata Singers of Ottawa.
- Values: artistic excellence, accessibility and inclusion, youth leadership, community.
- Vision: to become the most recognized, respected, inclusive and well-funded youth choir in Canada.

**Audiences, in priority order:**
1. Prospective singers aged 14–25 and their parents.
2. Concertgoers buying tickets.
3. Donors, sponsors, volunteers and board prospects.
4. Current choristers looking for logistics.

## 2. Brand system (from the attached ORYC Brand Guide, November 2021)

The brand is described as **"modern, young and playful."** Assets should be **photo- and people-forward**, use **simple graphic elements to complement photos**, and use **bold text and typography**.

**Colour palette:**

| `#AAC738` | Lime | Sparing accent |

**Contrast rules from the guide:**
- Good pairings: white on red, red on black, black on teal.
- Explicitly bad pairings: white on mint, black on deep green. Do not use these.
- All text must meet WCAG AA contrast.

**Typography:**
- Glacial Indifference only. The OTF files are attached and free under the SIL Open Font License.
- Titles: Glacial Indifference Bold, often uppercase.
- Subtitles and eyebrows: Glacial Indifference Italic. Only Regular and Bold are supplied, so use synthetic oblique or the Canva italic.
- Body: Glacial Indifference Regular.
- Headlines should be large and confident.

**Logos:**
- The wordmark "ORYC" has a translucent overlap where the O and R meet.
- Use the **emblem** (stacked OR/YC) in small spaces such as the header and favicon.
- Use the **full logo** (ORYC + "OTTAWA REGIONAL YOUTH CHOIR") only where there is lots of space.
- Never rotate or distort any logo.
- Alternate logos exist (Canada, 40th anniversary, Pride). The Pride logo can appear in a Pride Month banner variant.

**Graphic elements from the guide:**
- **Emblem shield/pennant:** the stacked ORYC emblem on a coloured shield with a pointed bottom (teal, black or deep green). It sits in the **top-right corner**, as on the posters. This is a signature motif: use it as the header logo lockup or as a corner tab on event cards.
- **Large partial red ring/arc** bleeding off the edge of a photo, plus small solid circles in teal, lime, deep green and black. The guide says **"circles should be used sparingly to ensure there is still a mature feel."**
- **Photos cropped into simple shapes:** circles, half-circles, arched tops. Never use hearts or stars.
- **Highlighted pull quotes:** large italic quotation with key words highlighted in a teal or white box behind the text, with an attribution line. Use this for chorister and audience testimonials.
- **Poster info block:** a big photo on top, with a mint panel below holding a large italic CONCERT NAME and then small italic labels (DATE / PLACE / INFORMATION) over bold values. Use this as the pattern for event cards and event detail pages.
- **Red cover treatment:** a black-and-white concert photo with a red duotone overlay and a giant white ORYC wordmark. This is the hero direction.

## 6. Design direction and constraints

- **Mood:** a modern, young, bold arts organization, with the polish of a professional ensemble. Think editorial concert-season site rather than a school club. Big type, generous whitespace, strong red moments, and rich black-and-white or duotone choral photography. Playfulness comes through circles, the pennant emblem and highlighted quotes, used with restraint.
- **Photography:** use the attached cover photos and templates as style references only. Every photo position is a labelled placeholder per section 8.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA throughout, visible focus states, a minimum body size of 16 px, reduced-motion support, and alt text slots on every image.
- **Mobile-first:** full mobile layouts for every page. Thumb-reachable Tickets and Donate on mobile, as a sticky bottom bar or in the header.
- **Bilingual-ready:** include an EN/FR toggle in the header. Leave room for French strings, which run about 20% longer.
- **Build target:** a modern stack hosted on Vercel. Produce a reusable component library alongside the page designs:
  - buttons, eyebrow + heading block, event card, bio card, quote block, pennant tab, timeline item, accordion, form fields, funder strip.
- **Integrations to represent:** Zeffy (tickets and donations), Mailchimp (newsletter), YouTube embeds, Google Maps, and the Choirbook member-login link.

## 8. Photo placeholders (required)

I will supply and place every photo myself. Do not use stock photography, AI-generated images or random fills. Every image position must be a clearly labelled placeholder that tells me exactly what photo belongs there.

**Placeholder format:**
- A neutral mint (`#DFEDEA`) or light grey box at the final size, aspect ratio and crop shape (circle, arch, half-circle, full-bleed and so on).
- A small camera icon.
- Three lines of text inside the box:
  1. **Shot type.** Use one of: Performance – Wide, Performance – Close, Rehearsal, Bootcamp / Social, Chorister Candid, Headshot, Group Portrait, Venue, Archival / Historical, Event Poster Image, Logo / Partner.
  2. **Description** of the exact shot, for example "Full choir mid-performance in St. Joseph's, conductor visible, warm light."
  3. **Specs:** aspect ratio, minimum pixel size, orientation, and any treatment applied in code (red duotone, black-and-white, crop shape).
- Each placeholder gets a unique ID (e.g. `HOME-HERO-01`) so I can match files to slots.

**When this design is built in code (Claude Code / Vercel):**
- Keep every placeholder as a reusable `<PhotoSlot>` component with `id`, `shotType`, `description`, `aspectRatio`, `minSize` and `treatment` props.
- The placeholder text must render on screen until I drop in a real image.
- Store image paths in one central file (e.g. `/content/photos.ts`) keyed by slot ID, so I swap each photo in one place.
- Apply the duotone, black-and-white and shape crops in CSS, so I only upload plain original photos.
- Every slot needs an `alt` field, prefilled with a draft description for me to edit.
